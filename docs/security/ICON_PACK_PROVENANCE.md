# Deck icon-pack provenance

Codecks maps actions to its own `ActionIcon` semantics. Pack switching changes appearance only.
No trademarked product logo is copied into the generic catalog.

| Pack | Dependency | License | Upstream | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tabler | `br.com.devsrsouza.compose.icons:tabler-icons:1.1.1` | MIT | <https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons> | Tabler contributors. |
| Feather | `br.com.devsrsouza.compose.icons:feather:1.1.1` | MIT | <https://github.com/feathericons/feather> | Cole Bemis and Feather contributors. |
| Material | `androidx.compose.material:material-icons-extended` | Apache-2.0 | <https://github.com/google/material-design-icons> | Generic Material utility symbols. |
| Rounded | `androidx.compose.material:material-icons-extended` | Apache-2.0 | <https://github.com/google/material-design-icons> | Rounded Material variants; no separate copied asset set. |

The repository redistributes compiled vector definitions through the declared dependencies. Their
license texts remain supplied by the dependency artifacts and upstream projects. The launcher robot
identity is separate and is not part of these generic Deck packs.
